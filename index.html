Passenger Door Drive Nut GO/NO-GO Gauge

SolidWorksPrototypingAnodized aluminum

Part Modeling

  • Designed a gauge to verify alignment of passenger door drive nuts on a leadscrew
  • Misaligned nuts will not enter the gauge

Prototyping & Testing

  • 3D-printed a test gauge and trialed it on a full train set of door leadscrews
  • Iterated the design based on test results
  • Found the printed gauge flexed enough to pass slightly misaligned nuts, requiring a metal build

Final Gauge

  • Released manufacturing drawing for fabrication in anodized aluminum
  • The rigid gauge enforces tighter alignment, improving passenger door quality

CoreXY Hobbyist 3D Printer

SolidWorksGD&TMotion design

Goal

  • Designed a customizable FDM 3D printer offering quality prints at lower cost than commercial options

Design

  • CoreXY kinematics with timing belt drive, lead screws and NEMA 23 stepper motors
  • Frame assembled with corner brackets for easy modification
  • Applied GD&T on all drawings

Results

  • Predicted 0.1 mm accuracy at 60 mm/s
  • Estimated build cost of ~$1,000 CAD, 60% below comparable commercial printers
